About CardioSense

Built with Purpose,
Powered by Science

CardioSense combines machine learning expertise with healthcare awareness to provide accessible cardiovascular risk assessment for everyone.

Our Mission

Cardiovascular disease remains the leading cause of death globally. Early detection and awareness can save lives. CardioSense aims to democratize access to AI-powered health insights, empowering individuals to take proactive steps toward heart health.

17.9M Deaths per year from CVD globally
80% Of premature CVD is preventable
